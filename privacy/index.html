Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Who I am

Halif Media is run by Abdul Ahad Rehmani, based in the EU. Privacy questions: privacy@meethalif.com.

What I collect

What you share in the contact forms includes your name, brand or channel, and email. Depending on the form, you can share a message or answer questions about your target audience and past YouTube sponsorships. I use it to reply to you.

You don’t have to share anything, but I can’t reply without your name and email.

This relies on my legitimate interest in answering messages and running and securing this website.

No ads, no tracking cookies.

Who else handles it

  • My email provider delivers and stores the email.
  • My spam-protection service checks your IP address and browser details to block bots.
  • My website host and network provider log IP addresses for security.

Some of these services are based in the U.S. and are certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

How long I keep it

Up to two years after we last spoke, then I delete it.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to how I use it. Email privacy@meethalif.com. You can also complain to Poland’s data protection authority (UODO) or the one where you live.
