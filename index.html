What I do

Know how every creator was vetted before you spend a cent.

For brands

Subscriber count alone doesn’t tell you much anymore. I’ve seen fast-growing channels where bot comments drew hundreds of replies calling them out. Engagement went up, but it wasn’t real interest. What real viewers say in the comments tells you more than any follower number.

I vet every creator and show you how, so the partnership works for both sides. Then I handle the rest: the brief, contract, timelines, approvals and a performance report after the video goes live.

For creators

You deal with one person: me. I’ve made content myself, so I know your channel isn’t ad space. Contracts, admin, payments and reporting are on me, and I keep both sides on the same page.
